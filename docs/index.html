What it does

UtiliScope reads the color of any pixel on your screen in real-time and plots it on a broadcast-standard vectorscope. It shows you exactly where your colors sit — and whether your skin tones, white balance, and saturation are where they should be.

Works alongside DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, Lightroom, Capture One, or any app. Just open UtiliScope, move your cursor, and read.

Features

◎

5 Colorspaces

BT.709, BT.601, BT.2020, DCI-P3, and ARRI Wide Gamut. The vectorscope targets, gamut outline, and skin line recalculate for each standard.

⏃

Skin Tone Line

The I-axis indicator used in Resolve, Baselight, and hardware scopes. Calibrated per colorspace. All human skin tones should fall on this line.

+

White Balance Crosshair

A center cross marks perfect neutral (Cb=0, Cr=0). Point at a grey card, lock the target, and adjust until the dot sits on the cross.

#

Live RGB, Hex, and Position

Exact pixel values updated in real-time. Copy hex to clipboard with V. Adjustable sample size from 1×1 to 30×30 pixels.

◉

Follow, Lock, and Capture Modes

Follow the cursor in real-time, lock onto a specific pixel, or capture a region for analysis. Anti-jitter filter keeps the reading stable in lock mode.

⊕

Markers and Snapshots

Drop numbered markers anywhere on the vectorscope. Take a snapshot to overlay before/after comparisons on the same scope.
